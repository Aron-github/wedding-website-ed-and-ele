How to get there

Let us know HERE if you need support in organizing your travels.
We would suggest you to reach the area on the day prior to the event, to avoid complications.
The easiest solution is to stay in Oderzo the night before the wedding, or at least the night of the wedding itself. Oderzo is indeed very near to the wedding location, and we have agreed some fixed fares with local accomodation options (see "Where to stay").
We will take care of providing a transportation from Oderzo to the Villa, in case you would have no auto.
For those of you who can't do without the city vibe, we suggest you to organize your stay in Treviso, the place is absolutely worth a visit!

Plane

The two airports to refer to are Treviso Canova Airport and Venice Marco Polo Airport. Both are served by national and low-cost airlines, and both have both car rental and means of transport to the city and the train station.

Car

From Venice, take the Cessalto motorway exit and follow signs to Motta di Livenza along the SP53. In Motta, use the ring road to join the Postumia state road (SR53) and continue to Gorgo al Monticano, then follow signs to Villa Foscarini. From Treviso, simply take the Postumia (SR53) directly to Gorgo al Monticano.

Train

The railway line offers both the Milan/Venice route and the Adriatic Barletta/Venice route. Both of these solutions take you to Mestre, from where you can then take a train to Treviso and/or Oderzo.
